Inside the calcite crystal, along the optic axis, the ratio of velocities of extraordinary ray to ordinary ray is:

```markdown
Along the optic axis of a uniaxial crystal, ordinary and extraordinary rays travel with the same velocity; hence the ratio is 1:1.
```
